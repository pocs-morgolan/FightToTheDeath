Fight To The Death: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Fight To The Death ("the game") is an Android game made by Morgo. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

What stays on your device

  • The nickname you choose, your settings and your local rank are saved only on your device.
  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID.

What is sent during online matches

Online play only starts when you tap HOST or JOIN. During an online match:

  • Your nickname, your rank badge and your live gameplay (movement, shots and score) are sent to the other players in your room.
  • This traffic goes through Photon Cloud, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games GmbH in its European region. Photon's privacy policy is at photonengine.com/privacy.
  • Your device may connect directly to the host's device. Your IP address can therefore be visible to the players in your room, and Photon processes it to deliver the connection.
  • Match data is not stored on any server after the match ends, and it is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its app data in Android settings, removes everything the game saved on your device. No copy is kept anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
